Eye Lock

Red dot — where the phone sees your eyes looking Green circle — get the red dot inside it and hold to lock Target ring — once locked, the circle follows your eyes: carry it here Blink — dot flashes white. Long blink (~0.6 s) drops the circle or skips

First, 5 quick dots teach the phone your eyes. The red dot keeps getting more accurate the more you play: every time you look at a green circle, it learns.

Camera-based eye tracking (MediaPipe iris landmarks). Nothing leaves the phone.
